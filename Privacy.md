# A2 Rack Privacy Policy

Effective November 19, 2026.

A2 Rack is an open-source Android app for processing live audio. A2 Rack does not collect personal data or send it to the developers. The app contains no ads, trackers, or SDKs for advertising, analytics, or tracking.

## Microphone and audio

A2 Rack uses microphone permission to receive audio from your selected input, such as your device microphone or a USB audio interface. It processes the signal on your device and plays it through your selected output. A2 Rack does not save or upload your live audio. You can revoke microphone access in Android Settings.

## Models, presets, and settings

A2 Rack stores copies of the amp models and cabinet impulse responses you import, along with your last session settings, in its private app storage. If you export a preset, you choose where to save it. A portable preset includes the selected model files. If you choose a cloud storage provider, that provider handles the export under its own privacy policy.

Android may back up or transfer app files according to your device settings. Clearing A2 Rack’s app data removes its local copies and settings. Files you exported remain in the locations you chose until you delete them there.

## Source code and questions

You can review the code or ask a privacy question through the [A2 Rack GitHub repository](https://github.com/djshaji/a2-rack). GitHub issues are public, so avoid including personal information in a report.
